About

One developer. One rule: your journal is yours.

ThymeLime started as a personal need: a journal that keeps up with real life — words, photos, voice notes, moods — without selling my attention to do it. No ads, no trackers, no engagement games. Just a calm place to remember your year.

Principles I build by

Offline-first, alwaysThe app works with no account and no internet. Sync is a feature, not a requirement.
Encryption where it mattersJournals are end-to-end encrypted. Voice transcription happens on your device — raw audio never leaves it.
No dark patternsStreaks and limes exist to help you build a habit — never to guilt you into opening the app.
Deletion means deletionOne action in the app removes your account and data. It's in the product, not buried in a policy.
