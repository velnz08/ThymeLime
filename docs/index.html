Everything a journal should be. Nothing it shouldn't.

Journaling that stays yours

Text, photos, drawings and voice notes — organized on a timeline. Your content is never sold, never used for ads, profiling, or AI training.

End-to-end encrypted journals

Protected journals are encrypted on your device with keys that never leave it. Not even the developer can read them.

Streaks, quests & Limes

Build a daily habit with streaks and quests, and earn Limes — the in-app currency you spend on themes and small comforts. No lootboxes, ever.

Shared, not public

Share a journal with the people you choose — strictly invite-only. No public feed, no discovery, no search across users.

Works offline

Write on a plane, in the mountains, on the metro. Everything syncs when you're back — built offline-first.

Make it yours

Themes, custom templates and daily reflection prompts. Free to start — Plus unlocks statistics, friends and the full toolkit.

Private by design, not by promise

The full privacy policy is written in plain language: what is collected, why, what never leaves your device, and how to delete everything. Voice notes are transcribed on your device — your audio is never uploaded for transcription.

Leaving should be easy too

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — no reinstalling the app, no dark patterns. Requests are honored within 30 days.
