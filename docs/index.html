FAQ

Short answers, no fine print

Does ThymeLime work without an account — or without internet?
Yes and yes. You can start journaling immediately, fully offline. An account is only needed when you want encrypted sync across devices.
What does it cost?
Free to start. The core journal is free forever; optional extras (like themes bought with earned limes or Plus perks) are clearly marked — no dark patterns, no ads, ever.
How do I delete my data?
From the app or the dedicated deletion page: one action removes your account and all associated data, permanently.
