Summary

1. Information we process

Account information

  • Email address and basic profile — received from your sign-in provider (Google) to create and identify your account. We never receive your provider password.
  • Account identifiers — internal random IDs linking your data across devices and services.
  • Authentication records — session tokens and sign-in timestamps, needed to keep you logged in securely.

Usage analytics

We collect product analytics to understand which features are used and to improve the app: . Analytics include pseudonymous identifiers and usage events (app opens, feature usage, purchases status). Analytics never include the content of your personal entries. If you are in the EEA/UK and wish to object to analytics processing, email from your account address.

Purchase data

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, a device push token is used to deliver them via Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging and Expo's push infrastructure. Disable notifications in system settings and the token goes unused.

Server and security logs

Our hosting providers process standard technical request logs (IP address, request time, user agent) for security, abuse prevention, and reliability. These logs are not used for advertising or profiling.

Location and weather (optional)

If you choose to add a place to an entry, the app — only with your permission — collects your precise location (GPS coordinates) at that moment to attach a place label (via reverse geocoding) and the current weather (your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch it) to that entry. Coordinates, place names, and weather are stored with the entry and sync with your account. If you decline the permission, no location is processed and the rest of the app works normally.

Calendar import (optional)

If you enable Calendar Import in settings, the app — only with your permission — reads event titles and times from the device calendars you select (e.g. Google Calendar) and materializes them as entries on your timeline. Imported events sync with your account like your other entries. You can turn the feature off and delete imported entries at any time; the app never modifies or deletes anything in your device calendars.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, browsing history, or data from other apps. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
